## 1. Scope

This policy covers the Localhold name, official logos, official application
identity and other source-identifying brand elements. It does not change the
MPL-2.0 license that applies to covered source files.

## 2. Uses allowed without separate permission

You may accurately state that software is based on, compatible with, or a fork
of Localhold Community; link to the official source; reproduce legally required
notices; and use the name in factual commentary, research or security analysis.

## 3. Uses that require written permission

You may not present a fork, binary, service, domain, account or publication as
official Localhold; use official logos or confusingly similar branding for a
distributed product; imply endorsement, sponsorship or security approval; or
use official signing/store identity without prior written permission from the
policy issuer.

## 4. Fork identification

A distributed fork must use a clearly distinct product name and application
identity and must not imply that its binaries, privacy behavior or security
review are controlled by the official Localhold project.

## 5. Open-source rights remain intact

Nothing in this policy restricts rights granted by MPL-2.0 to use, inspect,
modify or distribute covered source files. The source license does not itself
grant a right to represent a derivative product as official Localhold.

## 6. Registration and legal certainty

This policy does not claim that Localhold is a registered trademark in any
country. No `®` symbol or independent legal review is claimed. Applicable
mandatory rights and lawful nominative use are not restricted by this policy.

## 7. Revisions and permission requests

Each published revision remains identifiable by the revision above. A public
contact route for permission requests will be added before commercial beta;
until then, no additional permission is granted beyond this policy.
