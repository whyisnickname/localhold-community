# Localhold trademark policy — plain language

- Policy revision: `2026-08-27-2`
- Policy issuer: `whyisnickname` (public pseudonym of the individual project owner)
- Legal counterpart: [`TRADEMARK_POLICY.md`](TRADEMARK_POLICY.md)

## 1. What this covers

The code is open under MPL-2.0, but the Localhold name, logos and official app
identity are separate. This document explains how the brand may be used.

## 2. What you may do

You may truthfully say that your project is based on Localhold Community, link
to the source, keep required notices, discuss the project and publish research
or security analysis.

## 3. What needs permission

Do not make a fork look official, reuse official logos for a distributed app,
suggest that Localhold endorses your product, or use official signing/store
identity unless the policy issuer gave written permission.

## 4. How forks should look

Give a distributed fork a different name and application identity. Make it
clear that its binaries, privacy behavior and security have not been approved
by the official Localhold project.

## 5. Your open-source rights

This brand policy does not take away anything MPL-2.0 allows you to do with the
covered code. It only prevents a modified product from being presented as the
official Localhold app.

## 6. What we are not claiming

We are not claiming that Localhold is a registered trademark everywhere, and
we are not claiming that a lawyer independently reviewed this policy. Rights
that the law gives you anyway are not removed.

## 7. Updates and extra permission

The revision number lets you identify this version. A public contact for extra
permissions will be added before commercial beta. Until then, this document is
the complete permission offered for the brand.
